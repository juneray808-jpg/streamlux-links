Refund Policy

This policy explains how refunds work for digital purchases on StreamLux, including Lux Coins and creator subscriptions.

Last updated: July 8, 2026

1. Overview

StreamLux (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides digital goods and services through our mobile application, including virtual currency (Lux Coins), creator subscription tiers, and other in-app digital content. Because these are digital products delivered immediately upon purchase, refunds are generally not available except as described in this policy, as required by applicable law, or as provided under the policies of the platform through which you made your purchase.

This Refund Policy supplements our Terms & Conditions. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms.

2. General policy — digital purchases are non-refundable

Except where required by law or expressly stated below, all purchases of Lux Coins, subscriptions, and other digital items on StreamLux are final and non-refundable once the transaction is completed and the digital goods are credited to your account.

This includes, without limitation:

  • Lux Coin packages purchased through the app;
  • Coins spent on gifts, tips, or other in-app interactions with creators;
  • Creator subscription fees (Silver, Gold, Diamond, Premium, or other tiers);
  • Promotional or bonus Coins granted as part of a paid bundle.

By completing a purchase, you acknowledge that you receive immediate access to the digital content and waive any statutory right of withdrawal that may otherwise apply to digital goods, to the extent permitted by law in your jurisdiction.

3. Purchases through app stores

If you purchased Lux Coins or a subscription through Google Play or the Apple App Store, your transaction is processed by that platform. Refund eligibility, time limits, and procedures are governed by the applicable store’s policies:

  • Google Play: Refund requests are handled according to Google Play’s refund policies. You may request a refund through Google Play within the periods and conditions Google allows (for example, within 48 hours for certain purchases).
  • Apple App Store: Refund requests are handled according to Apple’s refund process and Apple Media Services Terms. Apple determines refund eligibility at its sole discretion in accordance with its policies.

StreamLux does not control app store billing systems and cannot override a store’s refund decision. If a store approves a refund, we will revoke the corresponding Coins or subscription access from your account when notified by the store or when we detect a chargeback or payment reversal.

4. Lux Coins

4.1 Non-refundable by default

Lux Coins are a virtual, licensed currency with no real-world cash value. Once Coins are added to your balance, they cannot be exchanged for cash and are not refundable, including if:

  • You change your mind after purchase;
  • You did not use the Coins;
  • You spent Coins on gifts or interactions you later regret;
  • Your account is suspended or terminated for violating our Terms.

4.2 Limited exceptions

We may, at our sole discretion, credit Coins or issue a refund only in these limited circumstances:

  • Duplicate charge: You were billed twice for the same coin package due to a verified technical error;
  • Non-delivery: Coins were not credited to your account after a successful payment, and we cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable time;
  • Unauthorized transaction: You provide credible evidence that a purchase was made without your authorization and app store dispute channels have been exhausted or do not apply;
  • Legal requirement: A refund is mandated by applicable consumer protection law in your jurisdiction.

To request review under these exceptions, contact support@streamlux.io within 14 days of the transaction with your order ID, StreamLux username, purchase date, and a description of the issue.

5. Creator subscriptions

Paid subscriptions to creators renew automatically until cancelled in your Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings. Cancelling stops future charges but does not entitle you to a refund for the current billing period unless required by law or approved by the app store.

  • No partial refunds for unused time in an active subscription period;
  • No refunds because a creator changed posting frequency, went offline, or you were dissatisfied with content—unless a store policy or law requires otherwise;
  • If a creator account is permanently removed for policy violations, we may offer a prorated credit or refund at our discretion—we are not obligated to do so.

To avoid unwanted renewal charges, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period through your app store account.

6. Creator earnings and withdrawals

This Refund Policy applies to user purchases of digital goods. It does not govern creator wallet balances, gift revenue, subscription revenue, or withdrawal requests. Creator payouts are subject to separate program terms, verification requirements, and admin review. Disputes about creator earnings should be directed to support@streamlux.io with the subject line “Creator payout inquiry.”

7. Chargebacks and payment disputes

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or payment provider without first contacting us may result in immediate suspension of your account and forfeiture of Coins, subscription benefits, and pending creator balances associated with the disputed transaction.

We reserve the right to contest illegitimate chargebacks and to share relevant transaction records with payment processors and app stores as permitted by law.

8. Regional consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws in your country or region. Residents of the European Union, United Kingdom, Australia, and certain other jurisdictions may have statutory rights that cannot be waived by contract. If you believe you are entitled to a refund under mandatory law, contact us with your jurisdiction and we will review your request in accordance with applicable regulations.

9. How to request a refund or billing help

  1. App store purchases: Start with Google Play or Apple’s official refund process (links in Section 3 above).
  2. StreamLux review: Email support@streamlux.io with your username, purchase receipt or order ID, platform (Google Play / Apple), date of purchase, and reason for your request.
  3. Response time: We aim to respond within 1–2 business days. Approved refunds for direct billing (where applicable) may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your payment provider.

For general billing questions, visit our Contact page.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted here; continued use of paid features after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
