Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 5, 2026

StreamLux is committed to protecting children and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards explain how we prevent, detect, and respond to such content and conduct on our platform.

Our commitment

  • We strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or activity that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or harms children.
  • We prohibit the creation, upload, sharing, solicitation, or promotion of CSAM in any form.
  • We prohibit grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any attempt to make inappropriate contact with a minor.
  • StreamLux is intended for adult users and is not directed at children.

Preventing abuse

  • Our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines prohibit CSAE and CSAM, and violations result in immediate removal and account termination.
  • We use a combination of user reporting and content moderation to identify and remove violating content.
  • Accounts found engaging in CSAE are permanently banned and, where required, reported to the relevant authorities.

In-app reporting

Any user can report content, profiles, livestreams, or messages directly inside the app. Reports involving child safety are prioritized and reviewed, and offending content and accounts are removed. We make reporting tools easy to find on posts, profiles, live streams, and chats.

Responding to and reporting CSAM

When we become aware of apparent CSAM, we remove it, disable the responsible account, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate local authorities, in compliance with applicable laws. We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.

Compliance

StreamLux complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the regions where it operates, and cooperates with law enforcement and child protection organizations as required.

How to report a concern

  • Use the in-app report option on any post, profile, livestream, or message.
  • Email our designated contact at juneray808@gmail.com.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services right away.
