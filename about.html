About StreamLux

We are building a global social platform where short-form video, real-time connection, and creator opportunity come together — designed for the way people actually use their phones.

What is StreamLux?

StreamLux is a mobile streaming and social discovery app that combines the best of vertical video feeds, creator profiles, instant matching, direct messaging, and video calls — all wrapped in a premium dark-mode experience tuned for performance.

Unlike platforms that treat creators as an afterthought, StreamLux was architected from the ground up with monetization, community safety, and fan engagement at its core. Users can explore trending content, follow creators they admire, match with new people, and build lasting conversations — while creators can grow an audience and earn through gifts, subscriptions, calls, and more.

We are in an exciting early stage. That means every user and creator who joins now helps shape the culture, features, and direction of the platform. Your voice matters here.

Where we are headed

Our long-term vision is to become the go-to destination for mobile creators who want discovery, community, and income in one place — without juggling five different apps.

  • Richer creator tools — dashboards, analytics, and payout flows that respect your time and earnings.
  • Live broadcasting — real-time streams with chat, gifts, and co-hosting (see our Livestream roadmap).
  • Deeper fan experiences — VIP badges, exclusive access, and premium interactions that reward loyalty.
  • Global reach — multi-language support and region-aware discovery so creators everywhere can find their audience.

StreamLux is not trying to be everything to everyone. We are focused on doing a few things exceptionally well: video that feels instant, matching that feels human, and monetization that feels fair. If that resonates with you, we would love to have you on board from the start.
