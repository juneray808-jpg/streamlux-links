Account & personal data · Android & iOS app

Delete your account & data

You can request deletion of your StreamLux account and associated personal data without installing the app. This page is provided for Google Play and other store listings that require a web link for account deletion.

Request deletion by email (no app required)

Send an email from an address you can access. Use the button below or write to support@streamlux.io with the subject line Account deletion.

Email: Account deletion request

What to include

So we can find the right account safely, please include as much as you can:

  • The email address you used to register (if you have a full account)
  • Your username or display name in StreamLux
  • Any other details that help us match your profile (for example, country, approximate sign-up timeframe) — do not send passwords or payment card numbers

If you still have the app installed

You may also be able to start account deletion from in-app settings (for example, “Delete account” or similar), where available. If you cannot use the app, the email process above still applies.

What happens after you request deletion

  • We will verify ownership of the account when needed to protect your data.
  • After confirmation, we work to delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable time, except for the limited categories described below.

Types of data we aim to delete or anonymize

When your account deletion is completed, we generally remove or irreversibly disconnect the following from your identity (subject to the “may be kept” section):

  • Account & profile: login identifiers we control for your app profile (such as email-linked account data), username/display name, bio, profile photo, and other profile fields you provided.
  • User-generated content (UGC): videos, images, captions, and other posts tied to your account; comments and similar interactions attributed to you, where technically and legally feasible.
  • Messaging: direct messages and related delivery metadata that we store for your account, where we can remove them without breaking lawful obligations or another user’s reasonable expectations (for example, another person’s copy of a conversation may still exist on their device outside our control).
  • Social graph & activity: follows, likes, saves, blocks, and similar in-app relationships and activity tied to your account.
  • In-app preferences: notification and privacy settings, language/display choices, and similar preference data stored for your account.
  • Operational call/session data: private 1v1 call logs or status metadata we hold that identifies you as a participant, where applicable and where we are not required to retain them.
  • Device/app identifiers in our systems: technical or usage records that are stored in a way that identifies you personally, where deletion or anonymization is practicable.

Some items may be anonymized (stripped of identifiers) instead of fully erased where that still achieves the goal of no longer associating data with you.

Types of data we may keep (and why)

We do not promise that every byte of information disappears instantly everywhere. The following may be retained for a period or in a limited form, consistent with our Privacy Policy:

  • Legal, safety, and disputes: information we are required or permitted to keep to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce our terms, resolve disputes, or protect users and the service.
  • Fraud, abuse, and integrity: limited records needed to detect or prevent fraud, abuse, repeat violations, or security incidents.
  • Financial & purchase records: transaction-related identifiers, amounts, or records needed for accounting, tax, chargebacks, or store/platform rules. Google Play and Apple (and their payment systems) may also retain their own purchase history under their policies—we do not control their records.
  • Backups & technical residue: encrypted or archived backups may take additional time to roll off; we work to delete or anonymize your data in active systems first.
  • Aggregated or de-identified data: statistics that do not reasonably identify you may be retained.
  • Content visible to others before deletion: another person may have saved or shared something you posted outside StreamLux; we cannot delete copies on other people’s devices or third-party services.

Guest or limited accounts

If you used the app with a guest or limited account and did not add an email, contact us at the same address and describe how you used the app so we can try to locate your data. Some requests may be harder to fulfill without identifiable information.
